THE QUIET

PATIENT 0-12
You wake up strapped to a gurney in the sealed sub-level of St. Ambrose Memorial. The power is failing. The bite on your arm has scabbed over. Someone has crossed your name off the patient list.

The hallways are full of the quiet ones: patients who stopped talking, then stopped sleeping, then got back up. You have no gun. Improvised weapons are scattered around: pipes, crowbars, an axe. They help, but they break, and every swing makes noise. Often the best fight is the one you run from, or the one you hide from: crawl under a gurney or desk, and hold your breath when they come looking.

Climb out through the ward, the maintenance tunnels and the ground floor. Find out what happened here.
