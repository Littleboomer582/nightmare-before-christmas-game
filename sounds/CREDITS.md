# Sound credits

Every sound effect in this folder is cut from CC0 (public domain) recordings published on
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org). CC0 needs no attribution, but these people made them:

| Used for | Pack | Author |
|---|---|---|
| Gunshots (1911, Carl Gustav M45, AR-15, AK-47, Mossberg and Nova shotguns, Savage 10, Mosin Nagant, S&W 642) | [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library) | bart |
| Zombie groans | [Zombies Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack) | artisticdude |
| Zombie groans | [Zombie moans](https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-moans) | Darsycho |
| Zombie attacks and deaths, player hurt and death | [Zombie / Skeleton / Monster Voice Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-skeleton-monster-voice-effects) | ArcadeParty |
| Footsteps on wood, stone, gravel, mud; zombie shuffles | [Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud) | TinyWorlds |
| Footsteps in snow | [4 dry snow steps](https://opengameart.org/content/4-dry-snow-steps) | qubodup |
| Reloads, shotgun pump | [Gun reload sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds) | SpringySpringo |
| Explosion | [Muffled Distant Explosion](https://opengameart.org/content/muffled-distant-explosion) | NenadSimic |
| Explosions | [25 CC0 bang / firework SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx) | rubberduck |
| Boards breaking, hammering, wood and metal bullet impacts | [100 CC0 metal and wood SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx) | rubberduck |
| Bullet hits on zombies | [8 wet squish, slurp impacts](https://opengameart.org/content/8-wet-squish-slurp-impacts) | qubodup |
| Boards breaking | [35 wooden cracks/hits/destructions](https://opengameart.org/content/35-wooden-crackshitsdestructions) | qubodup |
| Knife | [3 Melee sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/3-melee-sounds) | remaxim |

Rebuild this folder with `python3 tools/prepare_sounds.py <work-dir>` (downloads the packs, cuts and levels the clips).
Interface sounds, music, the Ray Pistol and the Arc Thrower are still synthesized in code.
