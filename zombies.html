Staying alive

Zombies tear the boards off the depot windows. Hold F at a window to rebuild it for points.

Spend points on chalk wall weapons, the mystery box, the debris door (750) and the lot gate (1000).

Throw the power switch by the generator in the bus lot to wake the perk machines and the Forge.

The route

Past the gate, a bus runs the foggy road between the depot, Pine Hollow Gas & Motel and Kessler Lumber Mill. Walk in through its side door before it leaves.

Thirteen parts lie around the route. Build the Riot Shield, Sentry Turret, Shock Trap and Gale Cannon at any workbench.

Other maps

Blackwater Pier: they climb out of the harbour onto the boardwalk. The power switch is by the lighthouse at the end of the pier.

Cold Hollow Mine: a snowed-in camp. The power switch is on the generator by the bunkhouse, and the Forge is at the end of the mine tunnel.

Wonder weapons

The mystery box can give the Ray Pistol, whose exploding bolts will hurt you up close until forged, and the rare Arc Thrower.
